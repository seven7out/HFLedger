# HFLedger community conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to an open, welcoming, and harassment-free project community. Participation should be safe regardless of experience, background, identity, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, design, and tradeoffs.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus on observable behavior and project impact rather than personal judgments.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's private information or operational data.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and make room for others to contribute.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory conduct.
- Sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting credentials, personal data, private paths, or real operational content to public project spaces.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing HFLedger or its community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on a maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed promptly, kept as confidential as reasonably possible, and handled without retaliation.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), including its emphasis on a welcoming community and proportionate enforcement.
